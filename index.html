About me

I design the interface, then I build it, so nothing gets lost in between.

I’m a senior front-end developer and UI/UX designer with more than nine years of experience building scalable web applications, enterprise dashboards and responsive digital products.

My work starts with business requirements and ends in screens people find easy to use. I build reusable front-end architecture, work with teams in Agile sprints, and care about accessibility, cross-browser support and performance.

Earlier roles as a software engineer and a data analyst taught me to work with APIs, business data and KPIs.
